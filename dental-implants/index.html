Dental Implants

A Lasting Foundation for Your Smile

Dental Implants in Markham, ON

Dental implants offer a stable, natural-looking way to replace one or more missing teeth. An implant is placed in the jawbone to support a custom dental crown, bridge, or other suitable restoration. At The Dental Hauz, we carefully assess your oral health and discuss your options to create a personalized treatment plan focused on comfort, function, appearance, and long-term care.

Replace Missing Teeth with Confidence

Missing teeth can affect chewing, speech, appearance, and the alignment of surrounding teeth. Dental implants are designed to provide dependable support without relying entirely on adjacent teeth. Once restored, an implant can look and function much like a natural tooth.

Treatment Planned Around Your Needs

Every implant journey begins with a detailed examination and appropriate diagnostic imaging. We evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and available bone before discussing your suitability, treatment stages, expected healing, and restoration options in clear, straightforward language.

Stable, Personalized and Natural-Looking

What to Expect from Dental Implant Treatment

Detailed Assessment

We examine your oral health and use appropriate imaging to assess the implant site, surrounding teeth, gums, and available bone before determining whether dental implants are suitable for you.

Personalized Planning

Your treatment plan considers the number and location of missing teeth, your overall oral health, restorative needs, and personal goals to support a functional and natural-looking result.

Implant Placement

The dental implant is carefully positioned in the jawbone. A healing period generally follows, allowing the implant to integrate and form a stable foundation for the planned dental restoration.

Final Restoration

Once the implant is ready, a custom crown, bridge, or other suitable restoration is attached. We carefully evaluate its shape, colour, fit, and bite for comfortable everyday function.

Support for Long-Term Success

Protect and Maintain Your Dental Implant

Dental implants require consistent care, just like natural teeth. Daily brushing, cleaning around the implant, professional dental visits, and healthy habits help protect the surrounding gums and supporting structures. Our team will provide personalized maintenance guidance and monitor your implant during routine examinations to help support its condition over time.

Our Commitment to Informed Care

We explain each stage of implant treatment so you know what to expect. Our approach emphasizes careful planning, honest guidance, comfortable care, and a restoration suited to your individual smile.
