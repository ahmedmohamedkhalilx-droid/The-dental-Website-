Questions

Good to know before you visit

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and the team will get back to you.

Do implants hurt?

Implant surgery is done under local anaesthesia, and sedation is available if you feel anxious. Most patients describe the recovery as easier than a tooth extraction, and we give you a clear aftercare plan.

How long does implant treatment take?

The implant usually needs a few months to bond with the bone before the final crown is fitted. In suitable cases we can place a temporary tooth on the same day. Your plan will show your exact timeline.

Should my wisdom teeth come out?

Not always. We recommend removal when wisdom teeth are impacted, causing pain or infection, or putting neighbouring teeth at risk. A consultation and X-ray will tell us which applies to you.

Are clear aligners right for me?

Aligners suit most adults and teens with crowding, gaps or bite issues. After a 3D scan we can show you a preview of how your teeth will move before you commit.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Every plan includes a written cost breakdown, and we can spread the cost of larger treatments into monthly instalments.
