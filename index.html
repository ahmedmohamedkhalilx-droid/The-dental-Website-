The Right Device For You!

Helping Patients Achieve Healthy Smiles With Confidence

Traditional Fixed

Traditional braces effectively correct crowded teeth, bite problems, and alignment issues, providing reliable orthodontic treatment for healthier, straighter, and more confident smiles.

Self-Connected Appliance

Self-ligating braces use specialized brackets designed to improve comfort while supporting efficient tooth movement throughout orthodontic treatment.

Invisible Device

Clear aligners offer a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces for straightening teeth. These custom-made, removable aligners gradually improve tooth alignment while allowing you to eat, brush, and floss with ease.

At The Dental Hauz, we create tailored clear aligner treatment plans to help patients achieve healthier, straighter, and more confident smiles.
