Orthodontics & Clear Aligners

A Straighter Smile, Designed for You

Orthodontics & Clear Aligners in Markham, ON

Orthodontic treatment helps improve the alignment of your teeth and bite for a healthier, more balanced smile. At The Dental Hauz, we assess your teeth, jaw, bite, and treatment goals before discussing suitable orthodontic options, including clear aligners. Clear aligners are custom-made, removable trays that gradually guide teeth into their planned positions with a discreet appearance.

Personalized Smile Alignment

Orthodontic concerns can include crowding, spacing, bite issues, or teeth that have shifted over time. Your treatment plan is designed around your individual needs, lifestyle, and expected treatment goals.

Clear Guidance at Every Stage

We explain the treatment process, expected wear schedule, home-care needs, and follow-up visits so you can feel informed and confident throughout your smile journey.

Discreet, Customized and Supportive

What to Expect with Clear Aligner Treatment

Detailed Assessment

We examine your teeth, gums, bite, and alignment concerns, using appropriate records or imaging to determine whether orthodontic treatment or clear aligners may suit your needs.

Custom Treatment Plan

Your aligners are planned around your unique smile, with each stage designed to gently guide selected teeth toward their intended positions over time.

Personalized Discussion

Scheduled visits allow our team to monitor your progress, answer questions, and provide guidance to help keep your treatment moving according to plan.

Retention Planning

Once active treatment is complete, retainers may be recommended to help maintain your new alignment and protect the progress you have achieved.

Confidence Begins with a Plan

Take the Next Step Toward a More Aligned Smile

A straighter smile can make daily brushing and flossing easier while improving the overall appearance and balance of your teeth. We take time to understand your goals and explain your options clearly, helping you choose an orthodontic path that works for you.

Our Vision for Your Smile

We believe orthodontic care should feel personal, clear, and supportive. Our goal is to help you move forward with a treatment plan that fits your needs and confidence.
