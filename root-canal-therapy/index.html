Root Canal Therapy

Relieve Discomfort and Preserve Your Tooth

Root Canal Therapy in Markham, ON

Root canal therapy is used to treat infection or inflammation inside a tooth while preserving as much of its natural structure as possible. When the inner pulp becomes affected by deep decay, trauma, or cracks, treatment may help protect the tooth from further damage. At The Dental Hauz, we carefully assess your condition and explain every step of your recommended care.

Saving a Natural Tooth

When appropriate, root canal therapy can remove infected or damaged tissue from inside the tooth, clean the root canals, and seal the space. The tooth is then restored to help support comfortable function.

Treatment Planned with Care

Before treatment, we review your symptoms, examine the affected tooth, and use diagnostic imaging when needed. We explain the process, restoration needs, and follow-up care in clear, easy-to-understand language.

Precise, Protective and Patient-Focused

What to Expect During Root Canal Treatment

Detailed Diagnosis

We examine the tooth and surrounding area to understand the source of concern and determine whether root canal therapy is an appropriate treatment option.

Thorough Cleaning

The inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and disinfected to remove affected tissue and help prepare the root canals for sealing.

Comfort Preparation

Your comfort is a priority. We explain the procedure, address your concerns, and prepare the treatment area before beginning the planned care.

Final Restoration

After treatment, the tooth may require a filling or crown to restore its strength, protect the remaining structure, and support normal chewing function.

Protect What Matters

Restore Comfort While Keeping Your Natural Tooth

A treated tooth still needs consistent care. Brushing, flossing, routine dental visits, and a properly placed restoration all play an important role in protecting the tooth over time. Our team will guide you through the recommended next steps.

Our Commitment to Clear Care

We make sure you understand your diagnosis, treatment options, and restoration plan. Our goal is to provide careful care that supports both comfort and long-term dental health.
