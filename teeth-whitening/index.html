Teeth whitening

Brighten Your Smile with Professional Care

Teeth Whitening in Markham, ON

Professional teeth whitening can help brighten teeth affected by common surface stains from coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and everyday foods. At The Dental Hauz, we assess your smile before recommending a whitening approach that suits your goals and oral health. We focus on helping you achieve a fresher, brighter-looking smile with clear guidance and personalized care.

A Brighter Smile, Planned for You

Teeth can naturally change colour over time, and whitening results vary from person to person. We discuss your expectations, existing dental work, and tooth sensitivity before helping you select an appropriate option.

Professional Guidance Matters

A dental evaluation is important before whitening because restorations such as crowns, bridges, and fillings do not whiten in the same way as natural enamel. Our team will explain what results may be possible for your smile.

Bright, Balanced and Personalized

What to Expect from Teeth Whitening

Smile Assessment

We evaluate your teeth, gums, existing restorations, and current tooth shade to determine whether whitening is appropriate for your oral health and goals.

Personalized Recommendations

Based on your needs, we discuss a suitable whitening option and explain the expected process, care instructions, and factors that may affect your results.

Comfort Considerations

If you have sensitive teeth or gums, we discuss ways to help manage comfort and recommend an approach suited to your individual situation.

Ongoing Smile Care

We provide practical guidance for maintaining a brighter smile, including oral hygiene, stain-conscious habits, and recommended touch-up care when appropriate.

Refresh Your Confidence

Reveal a Brighter, More Confident Smile

Regular comprehensive oral examinations are an important part of maintaining a healthy smile. They allow our team to track changes, recognize potential concerns early, and tailor your care as your dental needs evolve. At The Dental Hauz, we create a welcoming environment where every examination is detailed, respectful, and focused on your comfort.

Our Approach to Brighter Smiles

We focus on natural-looking improvement and clear expectations. Your comfort, oral health, and personal goals guide every whitening recommendation we make.
