Tooth-Colored Restorations

Natural-Looking Dental Repair

Tooth-Colored Restorations in Markham, ON

Tooth-colored restorations repair teeth affected by cavities, minor chips, cracks, or wear while preserving a natural-looking smile. At The Dental Hauz, we use carefully selected composite resin that closely matches the shade of your surrounding teeth. Each restoration is shaped and polished to support comfortable function, a balanced bite, and a seamless appearance.

Restore Strength Without a Metallic Look

Composite restorations bond directly to the tooth and can often be placed while preserving more of its healthy structure. Their natural shade makes them a discreet option for restoring visible teeth as well as many areas used for everyday biting and chewing.

Personalized to Match Your Smile

Your treatment begins with an assessment of the affected tooth and surrounding tissues. After removing damaged or decayed material, we select a suitable shade, place the composite in precise layers, and carefully shape the restoration to complement your tooth.

Precise, Comfortable and Natural

What to Expect from a Tooth-Colored Restoration

Careful Assessment

We examine the affected tooth and may use diagnostic imaging when appropriate to understand the extent of damage and determine whether a tooth-colored restoration is a suitable treatment option.

Shade Selection

A composite shade is selected to closely complement your natural enamel, helping the finished restoration blend with the surrounding tooth and maintain a balanced, natural-looking appearance.

Precise Placement

The prepared tooth is carefully cleaned before the composite material is placed, shaped, and hardened in layers to rebuild the damaged area while supporting the remaining healthy structure.

Bite Refinement

We check your bite and make necessary adjustments before polishing the restoration. This final step helps create a smooth surface and promotes comfortable biting and chewing.

Repair Designed Around You

Restore Your Tooth with Confidence

Tooth-colored restorations offer a practical way to address cavities and minor dental damage without drawing attention to the repaired area. With consistent brushing, flossing, professional cleanings, and mindful habits, your restoration can continue supporting your smile for years. Our team will explain how to care for the treated tooth and when it should be monitored.

Our Approach to Natural Results

We focus on restoring both the function and appearance of your tooth. Every restoration is planned with attention to shade, shape, bite, comfort, and your individual oral health needs.
